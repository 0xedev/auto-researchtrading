# Positioning

This repository is no longer positioned as a generic trading-research playground.

## Exact Game

We are building an autonomous, explainable, market-neutral AI hedge fund candidate.

That means:

- not a retail signal bot
- not a general-purpose quant research toolkit
- not a software product for other traders

The target outcome is a fund-grade system that:

- researches and validates strategies autonomously
- trades liquid macro and crypto instruments with explicit risk limits
- reports institutional metrics and explainable trade rationale

## Wedge

Our wedge is not "we also use AI."

Our wedge is:

- audited validation discipline
- reproducible research infrastructure
- explainable decision paths
- controlled migration from research to live trading

## What Counts As Winning

To compete credibly with AI-native hedge funds, the project must become:

1. a stable research control with preserved baselines
2. a live-capable execution and risk system
3. an explainable operator product
4. an investor-legible strategy with institutional metrics

## Non-Goals For This Repo

This repo should not drift into:

- broad consumer finance UX
- discretionary manual trading workflows
- constant baseline churn without preserved controls
- flashy claims that are not backed by holdout-safe evaluation
